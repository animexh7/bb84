Manufacturing siteChapter 2 of 5

What reaches the plant?

The plant doesn't know which basis the lab used, so it guesses one for every photon. A right guess returns the lab's bit. A wrong guess returns a coin flip.

With a counterfeiter on the line, these are the photons they re-sent, not the lab's originals.

Only the lucky guesses are worth keeping.

Key siftingChapter 3 of 5

Which bits can both sites trust?

Over an open line the two sites compare bases, never bits. Positions where the bases agree are kept. The rest are discarded.

Keeping them isn't enough. They still have to be checked.

Key verificationChapter 4 of 5

Did anyone listen in?

The sites reveal a random half of the sifted key and count mismatches. Anyone who measures a photon disturbs it, and that disturbance shows up as errors.

A key that passes can lock the formulation.
